# Motivation 
- Global research community lacks synchronized research vision between highly intertwined Algorithm,System and Hardware,Workload.
- Achieving not only advancement in performance, but also computing efficiency, **performance per efficiency** and **intelligence per energy** has become critical goal.
- **GLOBAL GOAL** : in 10 years, enabling self-optimizing system that spans cloud, edge, physical AI that achieves x1000 efficiency improvements in both training and inference. then how to realize it? 

# Main objectives 

## 1. x1000 Efficiency.
- co-optimziation of model and hardware. (including low-complexity high-quality model, cross-layer optimization, hardware-agnostic benchmarking frameworks => co-evolution of models/compilers/runtimes/architectures/devices) 

## 2. Design productivity and Adaptability.
- Not a single design point, the accelerator design should be evaluated on their **multi-dimensional pareto-surfaces**. Approaching pareto optimality via efficient methodology is important, this is why design methodology and evaluation framework matters.

## 3. Reliability and Trustworthy AI systems 
- Like latency-power, accuracy/robustness/efficieny are also in the trade-off relation.

## 4. Addressing core bottlenecks 
- Next-generation systems may include heterogeneous architecture consisting Programmable Fabrics/Quantum processor/AI accelerator/Interconencts, optimization should be performed in system-level, considering these collective effects.

# Hardware side 
## Insights 
- **System level constraints** such as power delivery, **data movement**, reliability, cooling have become limiting factor rather than chip level consideration.
- Hardware design process should be **AI-assisted, Adaptive**.
## Direction
- Cross layer, System-centric AI hardware design 
- Memory centric as a algorithmic enabler, 3d and heterogeneous pakaging for new dataflows.
- Connectivity-compute-topology co-design
